Make the most of OrcAgent

Live MarketPortfolio statisticsReferral earnings

Referral earnings come from collected platform fees under the existing referral program. Deposits and sign-ups alone do not earn trading rewards.

Only confirmed executions through OrcAgent count, valued at execution time in USDC equivalent. Each transaction counts once. Network fees, deposits, transfers, tips and failed transactions are excluded. Trades below 1 USDC do not count. Repeated trades in the same token within 10 minutes are excluded; opposite trades within 30 minutes are held for review. Activity tracking begins when this feature is enabled. Historical trades without execution-time evidence are not reconstructed.

Participation is optional. Trade according to your own plan and budget; you do not need to trade daily or increase your spending to use OrcAgent.
