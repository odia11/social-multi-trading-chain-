Publication settings

Every 30 minutes (:00 and :30) · Europe/Amsterdam. Live posts first: call milestones, new calls, trending tokens, the best call of the day, the most called token, the top caller of the week and a market pulse. A product text only when there is nothing live. OrcAgent never posts the same text twice; a slot missed for more than 10 minutes is skipped.

Always English. Tagged posts receive a comment; tagged comments receive a nested reply. Short greetings receive an English introduction to OrcAgent. Platform questions only, with a clarification reply for unknown topics. No private group replies, market predictions, account access or transaction execution.

Limits: two replies per user per 15 minutes, eight per user per 24 hours, 120 across the platform per 24 hours. A saved comment gets at most one automated reply.

Follow-up context stays within the same user's public reply thread. New wording is associated with an existing reviewed English answer after three distinct users clarify the same topic. Conflicting clarifications return it to review. No model training, new product facts or external AI costs. Automatic observations expire after 90 days.
