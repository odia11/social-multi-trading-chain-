How tracking works

Use Share call on Home, Calls or Following to create your personal link and card. A link opens the exact analysis and token, with a route back after connecting.

Tracking starts with this feature. A new account counts after its first proven wallet login. The first valid invitation is kept for up to 30 days in this browser. Existing accounts and self-invitations do not count; separate browsers or cleared cookies can prevent attribution.

Qualifying trades are confirmed buys and sells of at least 1 USDC equivalent, recorded by the trading engine after joining. Failed trades, deposits, tips and transfers do not count. Rapid repeated trades and reversals awaiting review are excluded. Volume is not platform revenue or a cash reward.
